About the evening

A meaningful role for every child

Students Performance Day is our annual celebration of learning, confidence and Islamic character. This year, every performance shows a value lived out — a real choice and its consequence: telling the truth, keeping an amanah, showing respect, serving others and persevering with patience.

Skills help a child succeed; values guide how those skills are used. Our hope is that faith is visible not only on stage, but in the choices, behaviour and service of every student throughout the evening.

Six chapters, six values

The story of the evening

The programme moves through six chapters, from our youngest learners to our senior students. Each one turns a value into something the audience can see.

  1. 1

    Faith & Gratitude

    Early years

    Saying Alhamdulillah and caring for what Allah has given.

  2. 2

    Respect & Kindness

    Early primary

    Listening, speaking gently and including someone who feels left out.

  3. 3

    Truth & Amanah

    Primary

    Admitting a mistake and returning what belongs to others.

  4. 4

    Responsibility & Self-discipline

    Upper primary

    Keeping a promise, managing time and finishing a duty.

  5. 5

    Justice & Service

    Middle & secondary

    Hearing both sides and helping others without humiliating anyone.

  6. 6

    Sabr & Excellence

    Senior students

    Trying again, working carefully and acting with humility.

The shape of the evening

What to expect

The doors open for a 6:00 PM start. The detailed programme, with arrival times for each class, will be shared with families closer to the day.

  1. Opening Qur’an recitation with its meaning, and a welcome.
  2. Six value chapters Performances by every age group, beginning with our youngest children.
  3. Prayer & rest interval A supervised break for prayer, water and rest.
  4. Reflection Student reflection and a values commitment for parents to take home.
  5. Recognition & closing Student recognition, a brief message, thanks and closing dua.
  6. Supervised release A calm, phased handover of every child to their family.

Quieter children and students who need adaptations are given meaningful roles too. A child’s fluency or family spending is never a condition for taking part.

Your child’s safety comes first

  • Students stay with their class and teacher in supervised holding areas.
  • Children are released only at the release desk, to an authorised collector with a matching pass.
  • There is no collection from backstage or the stage.
  • First-aid cover and a quiet space will be available throughout.

Photos & filming

  • Media permission is asked for separately from participation consent.
  • School coverage respects every family’s choice.
  • Please be mindful when filming or photographing other people’s children.
  • Highlights are reviewed against permissions before being shared.

Costumes & preparation

  • We prefer practical, modest and reusable clothing over expensive one-time costumes.
  • Please label your child’s essentials.
  • Rehearsals are kept short and regular, with rest built in.
  • Let your child’s teacher know early if they feel anxious about performing.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will every child perform?

Yes, In shaa Allah. Every consenting student will have a meaningful role on stage, with adapted support where needed. Solo and pair moments are woven into group items so that everyone takes part.

Where will the event be held?

The venue will be confirmed in the event guide sent to families, along with a map and arrival instructions.

How many family members can attend?

Each family will receive a pass allocation based on the venue’s safe capacity. Please let us know about any accessibility needs in advance, and avoid bringing unregistered guests.

Can younger children leave early?

Our youngest learners perform early in the programme. If an early collection window is approved, it will use the same verified release procedure as the end of the evening.

Is there a participation contribution?

Contribution details, what they cover, and how to request a concession will be explained in the consent letter. No child will be embarrassed or excluded over payment.

Will there be time for prayer?

Yes. The programme includes a supervised prayer interval, with suitable prayer and ablution spaces for students, staff and guests.
